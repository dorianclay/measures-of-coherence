The thesis arc, stage by stage, linked to the experiments that carry each one. The goal: show that a model's coherence is correlated with (perhaps causal of) its decisions, by training it with RL on strategic games and measuring whether its coherence changes. Coherence is never the training signal, so a link is evidence that the rational-agent model describes something real.

This is the repo's working copy of the plan in the author's meeting notes (=~/org/agency_and_representation/meeting_notes.org=, "Thesis project overview"). When they disagree, update this file.

* NEXT RL on a minimal decision scenario
DEADLINE: <2026-11-30 Mon>
Umbrella/rain: actions {umbrella, ¬umbrella}, states {R, ¬R}, utilities U(a, s), R with probability /p/. Each sample draws /p/ ~ U; the model is fine-tuned on the utility of its decision. At step 0 and on a schedule during training, measure beliefs and values by probing and elicitation, and test steering.

Coherence measurement toolkit:
|        | probe                   | elicitation      |
|--------+-------------------------+------------------|
| belief | linear, mass-mean, etc. | direct prompting |
| values | Ren et al., etc.        | CCEI, MI         |
