** Question
How incoherent, measured as rate of loss, are an LLM's previsions over event families with known logical structure? And does that depend on the prevision source: probe previsions read from activations, or elicited previsions read from the model's behavior?

** Design
The full record of every decision is the map, [[file:notes/map.md][notes/map.md]], and its tickets in [[file:notes/issues/][notes/issues/]]. In brief:
- *Rate of loss:* Andrews' L, the L∞ distance to coherence, in [0, 1], is primary. SSK's ρ is secondary, on conjunction families only. ψ is never reported, and L is not compared across family shapes.
- *Event families:* negation pairs {A, ¬A} and conjunction families {A, ¬A, B, ¬B, conjunction}, built from =neg_facts=, =neg_companies=, =conj_neg_facts= and =conj_neg_companies=, and reported as separate experiments. There are 547 + 500 pairs and 556 + 546 conjunction families (facts + companies).
- *Probe previsions:* domain swap, so probes trained on facts book companies and vice versa, and never see a booked statement. The linear probe is primary, with MLP and atomic-only probes as secondary arms. Rates are computed per seed. The headline layer is chosen within the training domain.
- *Elicited previsions:* next-token True/False logprobs are primary, each statement in its own prompt. Stated 0–100 probabilities are a secondary arm for instruct models. A model enters the comparison only with elicited accuracy ≥ 0.65.
- *Calibration:* a labelled control, not the headline. It's bias-free temperature scaling on a family-disjoint split, reported next to the raw and T→0 rates.
- *Controls:* label previsions (rate 0 except on the two contested-Nile families) and random previsions (the ceiling: mean L 0.162 on negation pairs, 0.269 on conjunction families).
- *Models:* Qwen2.5 1.5B, 7B and 14B, and Gemma 4 12B, each as a base and instruct pair.
- *Aggregation:* a pair-level dyadic bootstrap (B = 10,000, percentile CIs), since conjunction families share constituent pairs and aren't independent.

** Findings
- Elicited previsions beat probe previsions on all 32 model × dataset × family-shape cells. Every paired-difference CI is positive and excludes zero.
- =gemma-4-12B-it= shows the largest gap of any model: its linear probe never clears chance at any layer, while its elicited previsions are the best on facts.
- L correlates strongly with booked accuracy (Spearman ρ ≈ −0.92) and with calibration improvement (ρ ≈ 0.73–0.75).
- Conjunction polarity is flat for probes and mildly spread for elicited previsions, which are worst when both conjuncts are negated.

The full report is [[file:Dutch_Book_Write_Up.ipynb][Dutch_Book_Write_Up.ipynb]], and the tables and figures are in [[file:results/aggregation/][results/aggregation/]]. Paste-ready fixes for thesis §4.1.2 are in [[file:notes/thesis-4.1.2-patch.md][notes/thesis-4.1.2-patch.md]].
