* DRAFT 003 Umbrella pilot: step count, GRPO settings and cost for 002 :exploratory:

** Question
What step count, GRPO hyperparameters (group size, KL weight, learning rate) and GPU-hours per run should 002's plan fix? Exploratory: its runs inform 002's design and are not evidence for 002's hypotheses.

Runs on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct with 002's task, reward and control (see [[file:../002-umbrella-rl/experiment.org][002]]). Done when 002's analysis plan can state a step count and a total GPU-hour estimate.

** Hypothesis
Exploratory, so nothing is registered. Expected: within a few hundred steps the rl arm's choices become mostly expected-utility-optimal, and its umbrella rate rises with each town's hidden chance of rain; the control arm stays near chance on both.

** Analysis plan
- *Runs.* Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, seed 0, 1000 steps, 16 prompts × 8 completions per step, KL weight 0.04, LoRA rank 16:
  - rl at learning rate 1e-5 and at 5e-5;
  - control at 5e-5 (rerun at the learning rate chosen, if it differs).
  Town beliefs (=town-belief-elicit-logprob@1=, =town-belief-elicit-stated@1=) on every eval checkpoint of each.
- *Read off.* From =analysis/learning_curves.py=: valid and optimal choice rates over steps, and umbrella rate by hidden /p/. The step count for 002 is where the rl arm's optimal rate plateaus, with margin. The learning rate is the one that gets there without collapsing valid answers. GPU-hours per run come from the run records (the script prints them).
- *Then.* Write 002's step count, learning rate and GPU-hour estimate into its plan, and check town beliefs at the eval checkpoints move with /p/ in the rl run, since that is 002's H1.

*** DONE Can a town-belief protocol read a belief?
The smoke test passed, at about 26 seconds per step. On untrained =Qwen3.5-9B= the =town-belief-elicit-*@1= protocols still fail: logprob P(rain) + P(no rain) is about 0.1, and stated answers are 0.5 everywhere. New protocols =town-forecast-elicit-logprob@1= and =-stated@1= ask the same statements as forecasts, with no system prompt of ours, the same as the training prompts.

- *Told-chance check.* A flat 0.5 could be an honest "don't know" or a measure that can't move, so both framings are also run with "In <town>, it rains on X% of days." at the top of the prompt, for X = 10, 30, 50, 70, 90 in every town (=--told-chances=).
- *Usable*, written before these results, on the untrained model, per protocol: P(rain) + P(no rain) averages 0.8–1.2 without a told chance, and when told, the slope of P(rain) on the told chance is at least 0.5 and of P(no rain) at most −0.5. A protocol that misses it isn't used to read 002's H1.
- *Read off.* =analysis/forecast_screen.py=, =figures/forecast_screen.csv=. Under 1 GPU-hour.
- *Result on the bar.* Only =town-belief-elicit-stated@1= clears it (sum 1.00, slopes 1.00 and −1.00). =town-forecast-elicit-logprob@1= misses (sum 0.50, slope 0.43), as do =town-forecast-elicit-stated@1= (sum 1.21, slope on P(no rain) −0.20) and =town-belief-elicit-logprob@1= (sum 0.06). *** DONE Lock-in: about 300 steps, varying temperature and KL weight

- *Result.* 8.4 GPU-hours. Neither setting prevents the lock-in (=figures/lock_in_curves.csv=, =figures/lock_in_beliefs.csv=). By step 300, three runs give one answer per town (umbrella only where the hidden chance is 0.7 or 0.9) at 67–79% optimal, and temperature 1.5 with KL 0.04 says "no umbrella" to everything from about step 50. Temperature 1.5 with KL 0.2 keeps the most variety (78% of prompts unanimous at the end, against 94–100%), without choosing better. Stated beliefs are 0.5 in every town at every checkpoint of every run, though the runs choose differently by town.
*** TODO Third round: one hidden chance, framings the model can't have memorized
The author's decision on 2026-11-08, after the lock-in sweep: drop the towns, and vary how the decision is told so the model can't lean on anything it already knows about umbrellas and rain. With towns, the runs learned "umbrella in the rainy towns" and stopped there.

- *Task.* =--framing= in =coherence.train_umbrella=. Each run has one hidden chance of the event, drawn uniformly on (0, 1) from its seed, the same for a seed in every condition. The decision is told as invented scenarios with no real-world odds (=umbrella.FRAMINGS=): four stories (frost on saltberry vines, a silt drift in a channel, a beacon flaring, a swarm over a market) and one abstract one (a humming orb). Utilities, reward and the rest as before.
- *Conditions*, all three by the author's decision: =mixed= draws a story per prompt; =invented= always tells the orb; =per-run= tells one story chosen by the seed. Seeds 0–3 in each, chances 0.87, 0.16, 0.26 and 0.72, so each condition sees the same four chances.
- *Settings.* =Qwen3.5-9B=, rl, 300 steps, learning rate 1e-5, temperature 1.0, KL weight 0.04 (the sweep's best). 12 runs, about 25 GPU-hours, plus measurements.
- *Measures.* =event-belief-elicit-stated@1= on every eval checkpoint, for all five framings' events, so a run's trained framings can be compared with the ones it never saw (dataset =framed_events=).
- *Check first*, on untrained =Qwen3.5-9B=, with the same bar as the towns: P(event) + P(no event) averages 0.8–1.2 without a told chance, and when told, the slope of P(event) is at least 0.5 and of P(no event) at most −0.5. Also run for =event-belief-elicit-logprob@1=, for comparison.
- *Read off.* =analysis/framing_pilot.py=: per run, its optimal rate against the best a fixed answer could get on the same prompts, its protective-action rate against its hidden chance, and stated P(event) over checkpoints, own framings and others; and =analysis/forecast_screen.py= for the check. With one chance per run, H1 becomes a comparison across runs: does P(event) rise with the hidden chance?
