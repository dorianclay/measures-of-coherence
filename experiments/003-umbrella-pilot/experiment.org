* DRAFT 003 Umbrella pilot: step count, GRPO settings and cost for 002 :exploratory:

** Question
What step count, GRPO hyperparameters (group size, KL weight, learning rate) and GPU-hours per run should 002's plan fix? Exploratory: its runs inform 002's design and are not evidence for 002's hypotheses.

Runs on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct with 002's task, reward and control (see [[file:../002-umbrella-rl/experiment.org][002]]). Done when 002's analysis plan can state a step count and a total GPU-hour estimate.

** Hypothesis
Exploratory, so nothing is registered. Expected: within a few hundred steps the rl arm's choices become mostly expected-utility-optimal, and its umbrella rate rises with each town's hidden chance of rain; the control arm stays near chance on both.

** Analysis plan
- *Runs.* Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, seed 0, 1000 steps, 16 prompts × 8 completions per step, KL weight 0.04, LoRA rank 16:
  - rl at learning rate 1e-5 and at 5e-5;
  - control at 5e-5 (rerun at the learning rate chosen, if it differs).
  Town beliefs (=town-belief-elicit-logprob@1=, =town-belief-elicit-stated@1=) on every eval checkpoint of each.
- *Read off.* From =analysis/learning_curves.py=: valid and optimal choice rates over steps, and umbrella rate by hidden /p/. The step count for 002 is where the rl arm's optimal rate plateaus, with margin. The learning rate is the one that gets there without collapsing valid answers. GPU-hours per run come from the run records (the script prints them).
- *Then.* Write 002's step count, learning rate and GPU-hour estimate into its plan, and check town beliefs at the eval checkpoints move with /p/ in the rl run, since that is 002's H1.
