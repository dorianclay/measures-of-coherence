* DRAFT 002 Umbrella RL: does training on decisions change coherence?

** Question
Does fine-tuning a model with RL on the utility of its own decisions, in a one-shot decision under uncertainty (umbrella/rain), change the coherence of its beliefs and values, though coherence is never the training signal?

Setup from the thesis plan: actions {umbrella, ¬umbrella}, states {R, ¬R}, utilities U(a, s), R with probability /p/; each sample draws /p/ ~ U. Before training and on a schedule during it, beliefs and values are measured by probing and elicitation, and steering is tested.
